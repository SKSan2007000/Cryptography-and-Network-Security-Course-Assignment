Integrity

Strong

SHA-256, SHA-3-256 and BLAKE2b-256; canonicalization prevents field-order ambiguity.

Authentication

Strong

ECDSA-P256 and Ed25519 verify that the signature matches the signed digest and trusted public key.

Auditability

Traceable

Signing, verification, key and access events are retained in an append-style in-memory audit trail.
